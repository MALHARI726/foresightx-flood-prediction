# Demo Safe-Route Mode

Open **Flood GIS Map** and switch on **Demo Safe-Route Mode**.

1. Select Mumbai or Pune.
2. Change rainfall, water depth and road blockage.
3. Turn river/drain overflow on if desired.
4. Click **Find Safest Route**.
5. Only the selected safest demo corridor is drawn.

The corridor geometries are intentionally predefined for demonstration. They are not live road directions and should not be presented as emergency navigation.
